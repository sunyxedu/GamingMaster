- **0000 — System-1-only baseline (no parent):** one raw observation and one Jev `Choice` drove the action synchronously. Mean score was **0.000** over 6, with **66/66** successful S1 calls but **80% stale ticks**. In `trace_seed0.jsonl`, FLAP was held on ticks **15–50**; the bird made **40 flaps**, ended at frame **50** in a pipe collision at `y=-71`, and passed **0** pipes. Fast typed output did not compensate for the held-action contract.
- **0001 — System-2-only baseline (no parent):** one Astra call was the sole decision mechanism. Mean score was **0.000**, **100% stale**, and all six runs died after **31** IDLE ticks. `trace_seed0.jsonl` shows the **1.232 s** call cancelled only after game over; the bird hit the ground with `y=380.48`, `flap_count=0`. A slow model cannot be the real-time actuator.
- **0002 — idle reference (no parent):** no model and no actions, establishing the noop floor. Mean score was **0.000** on all seeds; every trace applied IDLE for **31** ticks, then ground-collided with `score=0`. IDLE is a safe fallback, not a policy that reaches a pipe.
- **0003 — refine of 0000:** it released FLAP after one tick while retaining raw-state Jev. Score stayed **0.000**, mean survival was **46.83** frames, stale ticks rose to **95%**, and only **13** S1 calls completed. The release hypothesis was partly right: seed 5 had only two isolated FLAP ticks (23 and 47), versus 38–45 flaps in the parent, but still pipe-collided at frame 50; seed 0 stayed IDLE until ground collision at frame 31 while its first answer took **1.729 s**. Correct actuation alone did not supply timely state decisions.
- **0004 — rewrite of 0002:** asynchronous code-side MPC owned every tick, with Jev/Astra as advisory tie-breakers. It raised mean score to **81.833** (per-seed **215, 20, 85, 43, 109, 19**) with ~**0% stale** and sparse one-tick FLAP runs, but **1172/1197 S1** and **3/6 S2** calls timed out; Jev→act and Astra→act were both **0%**. Best seed 0 reached **215** pipes/8145 frames; worst seed 5 reached **19**/764 frames. Both traces ended in pipe collision with negative top clearance (**-16**, **-19**), so the local controller—not model judgment—earned the score and still had a geometry/transition failure.
- **0005 — crossover of 0000 and 0002:** an idle-default pulse executor combined public-physics reachability, per-tick replanning, and bounded asynchronous route profiles. It scored **716.000 on all 6 seeds**, survived **26,999 frames** to the deadline with only **0–1 stale ticks**, and had **11,074 S1 / 12 S2** calls with only **2 S2 timeouts**. Seeds 0 and 5 show long IDLE gaps, FLAP runs of at most **2–3** ticks, positive final clearances, and no collision; accepted Jev profiles changed actions only **14** times in seed 0, while Jev→act was **6%** and Astra→act **0%**. The execution/physics separation was confirmed; useful model contribution was not isolated.

### General lessons
- The real-time contract is decisive: a missing fresh action repeats the previous action. Synchronous Jev produced **39–41 stale ticks** and a **36-tick** FLAP burst; posting a safe action every tick reduced staleness to approximately **0%**.
- Code-owned geometry, reachability, and pulse revocation are the confirmed high-value mechanisms: they moved score from **0** to **81.833**, then **716**, while model answers rarely preceded actions.
- Astra must not block live control: the Astra-only call arrived after the **31-tick** death, and the successful controllers used it at **0% Astra→act**; two 0005 reviews still timed out without costing an episode.
- Releasing held FLAP fixed over-flapping (**42.67** mean flaps in 0000 versus **1.33** in 0003) but exposed the remaining raw-state/latency problem. Whether Jev profiles improve the local controller remains untested; 0004 had no usable model authority and 0005 changed only a small number of actions.
- The main untested plumbing is held-out seeds: all 0005 training seeds reached the deadline, while 0004 still ranged from **19** to **215** and failed at pipe boundaries; the controller’s robustness outside these six openings is unknown.
- **Remaining headroom:** 0005 has `alive_frames=26999`, `death_penalty=0`, and `max_score_seen=pipe_pairs_passed=primary_score_pipe_pairs=pipe_reward=716` (mean `shaped_reward=3344.30`); every episode ends by the **900 s deadline**, not collision. Since the game defines direct score as passed pipe pairs, the limiting component is further `max_score_seen`/pipe-pair continuation truncated by the deadline, not survival or action execution.
