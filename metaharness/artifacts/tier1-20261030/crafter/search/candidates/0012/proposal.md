Hypothesis: the persistent missing achievement is a crop scheduling/parser failure. The controller successfully plants a sapling, but the adaptor exposes it as `plant_growing`; the parent normalizes that object to `plant` with `ripe=False` and then never revisits it until after the diamond objective. As a result, `eat_plant` is zero in every archived training candidate even when the run survives long enough to complete nearly everything else. Treating the only observed crop token as a periodically probeable resource should unlock the achievement without weakening the dependency chain.

Evidence from the parent traces is consistent across seeds. Candidate 0002 unlocks 21/22 on seed 1101 but never `eat_plant`; its map shows the same `plant_growing` cell at (10,48) from step 126 through step 135 and again at steps 450–459. Seed 1102 plants at (22,37) around step 98, still reports `plant_growing` at steps 428–437, and dies after collecting iron without an eat-plant attempt. The unique object vocabulary in all eight traces contains `plant_growing` but no `plant`, `plant_ripe`, `mature`, or `grown` object. Existing `target('plant')` therefore rejects every observed crop. The score table confirms `eat_plant` is 0.00 for candidates 0000–0009, while `place_plant` is usually 0.88–1.00.

What changed versus parent 0002:

- Preserve the raw crop token and record each plant coordinate's first-seen tick. A `plant_growing` cell becomes probeable after 70 game ticks, with an explicit 25-tick cooldown between failed probes. This uses only observations and bounded probing; it does not assume a hidden mature label.
- Add a code-owned `farm_goal` before iron/diamond expeditions. It routes to an old remembered crop and faces it before `do`; successful inventory/achievement deltas immediately end the goal. If the first crop disappears or is never edible, a second legal planting is attempted once when a sapling is available.
- Include plant ages and last probes in the compact Astra state so the adviser can choose a farm objective when it is useful. Safety, recipe verification, pathfinding, and asynchronous advice remain inherited from 0002.

Evidence that would confirm the hypothesis: traces should show `farm_probe` reaching a remembered `plant_growing` coordinate, followed by a food delta or `eat_plant` unlock, and the aggregate `eat_plant` rate should rise above zero without reducing early tool/furnace rates. A useful confirming sequence is plant placement -> at least 70 ticks of growth -> return and `do` -> achievement. The hypothesis is refuted if probes never change inventory/achievements and consume enough ticks to reduce rare-resource or survival scores, or if the crop object is not edible at any age. In that case the age threshold/cadence should be revised from observations rather than adding more blind planting.
