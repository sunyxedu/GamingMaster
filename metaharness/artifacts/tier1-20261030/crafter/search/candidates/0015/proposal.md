Hypothesis: the remaining failures are often caused by spending the last food or drink while the planner is still pursuing a long objective. Rare-resource conversion and cycle handling in 0010 address route stalls, but they do not suspend progression when survival has become the limiting resource. A code-owned vital reserve lock should preserve already-unlocked achievements by forcing a refill search before crafting, sleeping, or exploratory mining.

Evidence: candidate 0002 seed 1102 reached 16/22 achievements, then collected iron and continued a short movement loop while food fell from 6 to 3 and health fell from 5 to 0 by step 577; it never crafted an iron tool. In seed 1108, the run had 15 achievements and health 4 before the final hostile movement sequence ended at health 0. These traces show that the controller can be strategically productive and still lose the episode after its consumable buffer is gone. Existing <=5 urgency ranks food/water, but when no target is immediately visible it returns to the normal objective and keeps paying survival cost.

What changed versus 0010: after combat handling, `reserve_action` arms at food or drink <=3, clears stale exploration, and owns action selection until both are restored to >=7 or a 120-tick bound expires. It searches known water, cows, and plants with wider route limits; if none is known, it exposes local frontier tiles without crafting or following stale Astra advice. The existing verified recipes, rare-resource conversion lock, cycle escape, path bookkeeping, and asynchronous adviser remain intact. The lock emits trace events so its effect is measurable and cannot trap an episode indefinitely.

The hypothesis is confirmed if traces show reserve mode beginning before health reaches zero, higher worst-seed survival and more post-iron conversions, with similar early achievement rates. It is refuted if low-vital episodes usually have no reachable refill and the lock merely delays death, or if the threshold causes too much early exploration loss. The 120-tick expiry and release threshold make those costs visible and bounded.
