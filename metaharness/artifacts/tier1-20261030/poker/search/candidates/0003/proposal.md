Hypothesis: the fixed opponent's equity-to-action mapping is an exploitable measurement channel. Inverting its actions into a weighted range, then comparing response-conditioned EV across ordinary bets and large overbets, should extract more value than treating each turn as an isolated natural-language decision. In particular, a river opponent that calls using equity against random cards can pay off enormous value bets against our much stronger betting range. This is a fresh design, not a refinement of the leader's prompting recipe.

Archive evidence: the leader's weakest training trace, `trace_seed1103.jsonl`, ends at 1800.44s after only five actions and two settled hands (−550 chips, reported −5.50/required hand). Its last step raises AA to 400 and reaches a flop facing 600; the hand never settles. Seed 2207 ends after nine actions with three hands completed. The leader's apparent 47.33 mean is therefore dominated by tiny, unfinished prefixes. Routine play must run locally if an episode is to assess 100 hands.

Implementation: a standard-library exact five-to-seven-card evaluator supports a combination-based inverse policy. All legal opponent combinations are weighted by the likelihood of their observed preflop and postflop actions. On the river, opponent equity against its own uniform information set and hero equity against each combination are exact. Earlier streets use 40/48 sampled runouts per combination, exclude known cards correctly, and retain a likelihood noise floor. This deliberately models what the opponent believes, separately from what hero can infer. A one-response search compares checking, calling, folding, candidate raises, 2/3/5-pot bets and all-in. It chooses a common continuation against a reraise, avoiding a separate action for each hidden hand. Preflop uses wide button openings, price-sensitive defense and premium-conditioned equity for reraises.

Astra supervises at the beginning and at public-evidence checkpoints. It receives the current observation, range/equity summary, action EVs, response predictions and recent public outcomes. It can change a bounded opponent model and override a current legal action. Fresh sessions avoid the leader's growing conversation. Calls have 150-second timeouts (above the archive's 69-second worst), are caught on failure, and stop with at least 400 seconds remaining; at most five calls are attempted and the final call-budget unit is preserved. No future cards, seeds, game internals or external dependencies are used.

Every action is checked against fresh legal bounds, followed by `ctx.progress()` and the resulting observation. Rejection, repeated unchanged observations, calculation failures, or three unlikely opponent responses trigger an evidence-bearing Astra repair. Postflop calculations and action predictions are rebuilt after every observed transition. Public showdown outcomes are retained as selected evidence, never interpreted as unbiased hand samples. Garbage model output leaves the local policy running.

Confirmation: complete all 100 hands without illegal actions or errors; weak independent match scores improve, with substantial profit from strong hands receiving loose calls to larger bets. Prediction calibration should improve or remain stable across audits. Refutation: large overbets mostly fold out worse hands or expose reverse implied odds; repeated low-probability responses show the documented opponent prior is stale; negative independent match scores despite completion refute the value hypothesis. An increase in completed hands alone confirms the throughput intervention, not poker strength. Uncertainty is across match seeds, not across correlated hands.

Known approximation: before river, response EV truncates future betting and applies a realization discount. Monte Carlo errors are correlated within a board; likelihood smoothing is a robustness device, not a posterior confidence interval. The supplied study has conflicting 96/512 sample references and mentions a later blocker-semibluff revision, so numerical opponent thresholds remain hypotheses under observation.
