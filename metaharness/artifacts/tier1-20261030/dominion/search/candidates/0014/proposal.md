Hypothesis: fixed local Action priorities leave current-turn money and scoring opportunities unrealized. Full-game purchase search can inherit that weakness through its continuation policy. Test the turn executor itself, using the reliable 0001 money policy as parent rather than refining 0011's purchase-rollout recipe.

Evidence: the leader 0011's weakest training result is -13 (1400), and 1101 loses by 3. In `0011/trace_seed1101.jsonl`, decisions 7, 11 and 33 choose Militia over Smithy; the local executor gives Militia a constant +27 and Smithy +22 regardless of live buying thresholds. These choices are opportunities to test, not proof that Smithy wins: the first archive counterfactual checks actually retain Militia in all three. A stronger parent example is `0001/trace_seed1401.jsonl`, decision 18: one Province remains, the agent is down six VP, and its hand is Estate, Militia, Province, Silver, Smithy. Ownership minus known hand/discard/play gives a seven-card deck containing Copper×4, Gold×2, Silver. The parent chooses Militia, producing $4. Smithy guarantees at least $5 and sometimes reaches Province money. Candidate 0014 selects Smithy. This establishes an immediate opportunity cost, not a counterfactual game victory, because Militia also attacks. The leader's 1400 loss also never acquires Gold; that separate acquisition limitation is deliberately outside the main hypothesis.

Implementation: retain 0001's acquisition and routine nested-choice policy. Add public-zone one-turn search for Action choices, Chapel trash subsets and Cellar discard subsets. Own unrevealed deck composition is public ownership minus hand/discard/in-play, checked against deck_count; only its hypothetical order is sampled. Known discard and shuffle timing are preserved. The bookkeeping/forward-card helper is adapted from 0011, but there are no full-game rollouts or purchase-policy searches. Common estimator samples are unrelated to simulator RNG and no seed or kingdom ID selects strategy. Other nested prompts use the baseline, and every actual step receives fresh occurrence IDs. A hypothetical world never commits an imagined draw sequence to the game.

Each first choice receives up to 96 samples, with paired uncertainty against the baseline; the search stops after at least 24 samples and approximately four seconds. Chapel subsets preserve at least four units of total Treasure money. At the Action prompt, hypothetical Chapel anticipates a sensible affordable trash subset; evaluating it with the inherited “trash all eligible Copper” choice would incorrectly reject playing Chapel even when it could trash just an Estate. The utility combines purchases made by the *same local buying policy*, gains, early thinning and attack bonuses. It does not credit an optimal imaginary buy bundle the executor will never choose. Terminal-pile remaining buys use exact point arithmetic, including Gardens thresholds and free Copper, while ordinary strategic utilities remain heuristics. Ending is checked after the full real turn by the game.

Astra sees current rules, public zones, current and most recent tactical alternatives, recent purchases, cash-forecast residuals and failure evidence. It can revise acquisition targets and attack/thinning weights, disable search, or repair the current prompt with validated IDs. Scheduled reviews occur at opening, turn eight and the first four-Province transition, with a fourth call reserved for recovery. Each request has a 300-second model timeout and a separately bounded 320-second future wait, above the recent 151-second worst and historical 216-second worst observed model latency. Unresolved futures stay tracked; one fresh failure request may proceed behind a stuck request. Late replies may revise strategic fields but cannot reuse old occurrence IDs. Model/parse failures retain a complete legal local controller. Remaining wall time and call budget are checked before inference; no more than four calls are launched.

After every actual action, ctx.progress(), decision advancement, illegal counts and expected acquisitions are checked. The first buy prompt of a turn audits the latest sampled cash forecast. Two envelope misses disable the estimator and request Astra repair; three consecutive sub-$3 turns also request strategic replanning even if every action was legal. Unknown card types/prompts and public VP discrepancies trigger review. The sampled cash envelope is neither a calibrated confidence interval nor a forecast of final VP; it should not be compared to current VP margin. A model-disabled estimator still finishes the game with the local policy.

Confirmation: improved terminal margins on weak seeds, legal completion, and changes at terminal collisions or trash choices backed by observed current-turn cash. Refutation: worse margins, persistent cash forecast failures, or a short-horizon utility that delays necessary thinning to collect immediate money. Attack bonuses are approximate, opponent hidden hand assignments are exchangeable, future action choices use the baseline, and no long-run engine value is simulated. Supported card names use disclosed standard Base effects; public zone/score checks and observed cash offer failure detection, not proof that every approximation is correct.

Verification: preflight passed. Archive-only checks produced legal answers to 1,270 pending prompts and conserved owned zones and nonnegative supply in 1,171 hypothetical turns. Focused fake-context checks cover malformed JSON, non-finite weights, TimeoutError and RuntimeError, rejected-action recovery, and stale/duplicate option IDs. Archive counterfactual searches are diagnostics only and are not new game evaluations. Trace actions are matched against the preceding observation: a step's embedded observation is the *next* prompt.

First dev evaluation (`dev/20261031-102640`): +9/+1, mean +5, both game_done, no errors, illegal actions, fallbacks or truncations. These match parent 0001's recorded +9/+1 dev results. None of the 20 searched action decisions changed the baseline, so the run validates execution and cash forecasts, not the hypothesis's scoring gain. One Astra review returned and modestly increased attack/thinning weights; two requests reached the independent wait cutoff. All 17 audited first-buy cash values lay within their sampled ranges. Following archive diagnostics, the final estimator corrects Chapel's nested continuation, values the actual local purchase continuation, and includes the latest tactical comparison in later reviews. These are consistency fixes; no training evaluation or held-out inspection was run.

Second and final dev evaluation (`dev/20261031-103807`): +9/+3, mean +6, both completed with zero errors/illegal choices/fallbacks/truncations, taking 321/608 seconds. There were four requests across the games, two returned plans, and two independent wait cutoffs. In the second seat Astra raised the total Militia target from one to two; the resulting acquisition path differed and eventually collected Gardens points. None of the 18 tactical selections changed that run's local baseline; all 17 audited cash outcomes stayed inside their sampled envelopes. Thus the modest +1 mean over the parent's recorded dev result is evidence for a functioning adaptive model/executor interface, not confirmation that tactical search improves final scores. The first game's opening call did not return, so its +9 still measures fallback execution.

Final source additionally includes the prior accepted plan in review context, discards a forecast when a direct model repair or rejected-action fallback changes the action being forecast, and values action gains against ownership *before* gaining (otherwise a just-reached support cap or Gardens boundary can distort its value). These consistency corrections passed final preflight, archive zone/legality checks and focused recovery checks; no third dev evaluation was run. The broad archive tactical sweep validated 434 responses and found 42 departures from the inherited executor, including the guaranteed Smithy-spending example. No improvement claim is made for hypothetical choices without actual game outcomes. The remaining experiment is for the evaluator's training and unseen games.
