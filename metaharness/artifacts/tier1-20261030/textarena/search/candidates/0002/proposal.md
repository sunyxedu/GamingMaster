Hypothesis: the naive leader's weakest Mastermind outcomes come from an untracked posterior and speculative final guesses under a very short horizon. A different objective from standard entropy or average-reward search—maximize the guaranteed terminal partial score, then expected score—can lift the lower tail and improve this small-seed benchmark without any seed-specific knowledge. This is a fresh robust-control design, not a refinement of the leader's prompt.

Evidence: in `0000-tier1_astra_naive/trace_seed15.jsonl`, `[1 2 3 4]` receives `1 black peg(s), 2 white peg(s)` and `[1 3 2 5]` receives `0 black peg(s), 2 white peg(s)`. The final `[4 2 3 6]` receives zero black and three white, ending at 0.375. The leader keeps no explicit belief or reward model. Its four TicTacToe episodes already draw, so further strategic gains must come from Mastermind; the 217-second call on seed 17 also demonstrates the cost of asking Astra to do arithmetic.

Implementation: enumerate all 360 legal secrets using only the public four-distinct-digits rule. Parse and retain exact feedback, reject contradictions, and filter the full belief. On the second guess, enumerate every legal probe, every feedback partition, and each partition's best final action. Rank final actions by minimum reward across consistent secrets, then total reward, then solve probability. Rank probes by the minimum attainable branch reward, then total reward of those branch policies, then solves. The first guess is a canonical representative of the symmetric initial action space. The final guess may be an eliminated secret if that better guarantees partial reward. The policy is dynamically maximin at every posterior; it does not claim to maximize Bayes expected reward. Uniform-belief averages are only tie breaks.

TicTacToe uses exact minimax over the public board and learner seat. Both solvers commit one move at a time. Every move checks `ctx.progress()`, rejection counters, native move advancement, consumed legal actions, and either board effects or feedback consistency. A discrepancy hands the failure evidence and authoritative current observation to Astra and keeps subsequent play under model control. Recovery validates the returned action against current legality; garbage and caught timeouts fall back to a legal move. The recovery timeout is 360 seconds, above the archived 217-second maximum. There are no normal-path model calls, no seed access, and no private-state inspection.

Confirm/refute: confirm if the minimum Mastermind score rises above the leader's 0.375 with competitive or improved mean, no invalid moves, and exact TicTacToe draws. Refute the objective choice if its average reward loses to an expected-value policy despite a better floor; refute the implementation if any native clue/transition causes false recovery or any actual discrepancy goes undetected. Full-space synthetic tests can quantify the floor and expected reward without touching validation or held-out seeds. Dev evaluation and pre-flight results will be recorded below.

It checked all 129,600 feedback/reward table entries against a separately expressed counting formula and 684 reachable TicTacToe states across both seats and every opponent choice, with no learner loss.

Interpretation: exact bookkeeping and search generalize across the full public code space, but the strong claim that maximin eliminates all 0.375 outcomes is refuted by enumeration. Difficult zero-black opening branches still have a 0.375 floor. This candidate deliberately retains the robust objective to test it against the parallel expected-value direction; it does not tune probes, tie breaks, or digits to observed development outcomes. A mean-score loss against an expected-value candidate would favor that objective for future iterations, even if robust control improves particular weak posteriors.
