## Hypothesis

The cold-start failure is primarily an execution-interface failure amplified by model latency, not evidence that Astra cannot plan a factory. A declarative, observation-driven goal compiler can execute the full turn horizon cheaply, leaving Astra to diagnose exceptions. This tests a different control abstraction from repairing the baseline's one-action-per-model-call recipe.

Evidence: baseline seed 404's first model answer was `place burner-mining-drill 41 34 4; place stone-furnace 43 34; handcraft iron-gear-wheel 1; mine_resource 1 20`, while the posted action was only `mine_resource 1 20`. Later the answer `place stone-furnace 43 34` became `place`; the observation reported `Invalid action grammar; see action_spec`. Its final observation was still turn 1, with the original furnace and drill in stock. Seed 303 similarly ended at turn 3. All four episodes scored zero and expired by wall deadline.

## Design

The fresh controller owns a seed-independent sequence of complete production milestones: direct burner drill/furnace pairs for iron and copper, steam electricity, a lab and Automation, then additional iron/coal supply and three red-science assemblers with three laboratories. It compiles stock targets from actual enabled recipes, including ingredient reservation and output multiplicity. Affordable common components are batched; independent placements share a turn. A per-turn steward extracts products, distributes coal, feeds labs and assemblers, and selects accessible red-only research. Coordinates derive from observed resource tiles and a rotated layout relative to the validated pump site. Trees overlapping construction footprints are mined before placement; wood gathering preferentially clears future sites.

Astra sees a compact economic/diagnostic observation only when progress stalls or operations fail. It returns a short JSON repair plan; strict game-specific parsing preserves complete hyphenated names and semicolon batches. The advisor runs through `ctx.spawn` and `ctx.think`, with caught call/parse failures. The foreground continues servicing while waiting and discards advice after a milestone change or more than 16 turns. At most five calls are attempted; no model call is required for ordinary servicing. The background task performs one budgeted model call, not an autonomous loop. No arbitrary generated code runs.

Each operation batch has a local stock reservation and a 100-item shared transfer ledger. Jobs wait for observed completed inputs. Explicit action errors and prolonged unchanged goals trigger diagnosis; every gameplay batch is followed by a new observation. Malformed model output is ignored rather than turned into a bare command. Full controller snapshots are logged because the evaluator's normal map observations can be truncated before machine details.

## Experiments and refinements

- Initial preflight passed: score 5.76, 36 actions, no model calls. Direct drill-to-furnace production and observed inventory roles worked.
- First dev attempt, seed 9001 (`dev/20261030-000321/`), verified 18 valid charged turns and score 1.86, then remained inside the first synchronous advisory call. It was interrupted after the second dev run finished; it has a partial trace and no completed evaluation. A six-turn unchanged construction target was too eager a diagnosis trigger. The final design uses a 12-turn trigger and asynchronous advice, so model latency cannot stop ordinary factory operations.
- Second and final allowed dev attempt, seed 9002 (`dev/20261030-001033/`), completed all 128 turns in 212 seconds with one System-2 call, zero errors, and score 18.85. This version's entire score was bootstrap: a boiler with water and 165°C steam reported `full_output`, while the engine reported `no_input_fluid`. The missing steam connector prevented lab power and Automation. Its score is not the score of the final revised harness.
- The final layout adds the missing one-tile steam pipe; this is an evidence-based geometry correction. Moving excess raw-material expansion after first science, batching already-affordable intermediates, and combining independent placements also removes startup overhead. Revised preflight passed with score **64.77 in 36 actions**, zero System-2 calls. Since bootstrap is capped at 34, this verifies real science scoring after the power correction; it is not a full-episode dev estimate.
- Footprint checks against the six archived initial observations found no terrain/bounds conflicts, but did find tree overlaps in several layouts. The final controller mines those blockers. The final post-clearing preflight also passed: **64.93 points, 37 actions, zero model calls**. Targeted checks verified footprint clearing, recipe multiplicity and immediate stock reservation. Action-grammar checks preserve the baseline's complete multicommand answer and reject malformed/quoted commands, mixed read-only batches, non-string outputs and arbitrary text.

## Confirmation and remaining uncertainty

Confirmation means early genuine plate production, substantially more charged turns per model call, real powered red-science production/consumption, and no unhandled errors. Refutation means deterministic geometry repeatedly blocks power or direct feed, the recipe compiler deadlocks despite resources, servicing starves infrastructure, or investment leaves too little productive horizon. Evidence supports execution throughput and the corrected power chain; full-horizon science throughput of the final revision remains unevaluated because both permitted dev attempts were used.

The experiment deliberately specializes in red science rather than building a green-science supply chain. Remote transfers remain a throughput ceiling. The fixed coastal layout is rotated, but complex unforeseen shoreline obstructions still depend on diagnosis. Long-latency advice may be discarded before it can help; deterministic servicing and compilation are therefore the core policy, and Astra is an exception mechanism.
