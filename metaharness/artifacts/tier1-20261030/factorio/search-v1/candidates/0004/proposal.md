## Hypothesis and archive evidence

The leader reliably completes the episode, but its factory is sized for producing red science rather than realizing the much larger laboratory consumption reward. More laboratories should recover stranded score; after that, green science should open additional valuable research demand.

In the parent's weakest training episode (`trace_seed202.jsonl`, score 2232.06), three working red assemblers produce about nine packs per turn, while three laboratories consume only three per turn on late research. The controller reports 78 red packs in stock at turn 100, 138 at turn 110, and 232 at turn 127, plus nine packs in each lab. Research `physical-projectile-damage-1` advances from 0.15 at turn 110 to 0.66 at turn 127. Iron simultaneously falls from 137 plates at turn 70 to 17 at turn 110. This identifies consumption capacity and iron supply as concrete bottlenecks, rather than model reasoning or turn utilization.

## Final design

The parent's verified bootstrap, direct burner/furnace pairs, pump-relative power geometry, recipe compiler, full-command parser, and asynchronous exception advisor remain the foundation. The extension builds a third iron smelting pair, a second coal drill/chest pair, one dedicated green-science assembler, and three additional laboratories: six labs, three red assemblers, and one green assembler in total. Coordinates derive from the current observed pump and resource tiles, never seed identifiers.

The controller assigns explicit recipes by machine position, compiles green intermediates from observed recipes, reserves gears for green production, and batches new labs in groups of three. A construction barrier completes the lab batch before commissioning green production. This prevents recurring inserter production from spending the gears and circuits needed to finish the laboratories. The second coal pair reduces competition between fuel gathering and intermediate crafting on the single manual worker.

The transfer steward collects science and fuels machines before extracting surplus raw materials. Burner refills are batched below a safe stock threshold instead of issuing one-coal top-ups to every machine every turn; this preserves operation slots for science. It fills the least-stocked labs first with buffers based on the selected research duration. Copper collection stops at a working reserve rather than spending transfers accumulating hundreds of plates. Every batch retains the parent's stock reservation and shared 100-item transfer ledger.

Research selection admits red/green technologies when green packs are buffered, preferring their larger consumption reward. It resumes red-only work when a green research has lacked available packs for two observations. Hysteresis avoids switching on every individual pack. Production, consumption, research choices, stock, machine status, and planned transfers are logged for later diagnosis. Astra remains an exception advisor with compact fresh state, bounded action parsing, stale-plan rejection, and caught model/parse failures; normal production does not wait for inference.

## Development evidence

2. First dev evaluation, seed 9001 (`dev/20261030-011807/`), completed all 128 turns without error at **2709.01**. This preliminary version built nine laboratories and no green assembler. The exact observed green recipe uses one inserter and one belt, six seconds per craft. ## Confirmation and refutation

Confirm if the extra laboratories consume more of the available science, green packs are genuinely engine-produced and consumed, and the combined score exceeds the parent without errors. Refute if construction or manual-worker contention starves existing production, the transfer quota prevents feeding both colors, electrical capacity is insufficient, research switching wastes available science, or green investment fails to pay back within 128 turns. Two dev evaluations are the limit; remaining uncertainty belongs to the evaluator's training run, not additional private experiments.
