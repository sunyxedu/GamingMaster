Hypothesis: science consumption depends on the active technology accepting the packs the factory can actually supply. The leader’s policy already gathers exact pack inventory and production ledgers, but its research gate treats any truthy `research.current` as a named, active project. An opaque sentinel or alternate observation shape can therefore freeze research selection while labs report `missing_science_packs`; a plan may keep making packs without scoring the high-value consumption term. The archive shows this matters at scale: on seed 202, by turn 80 the controller had produced 193 automation and 32 logistic packs, while its four labs had consumed 185 and 31, and individual labs were often waiting for missing packs.

What changed vs. the parent: retain its production compiler and normal healthy path. Make research state parsing tolerant of string, object, list, and opaque `current` values; only a recognizable, incomplete technology blocks selection. Normalize completed research from either mapping or list shape, then choose among currently attainable research using the existing weighted science-rate ordering and explicit early unlock priorities. Keep the leader’s bounded recovery behavior for actual operation failures, and use `ctx.progress()` to expose stagnation evidence to that repair path.

Evidence that would confirm or refute it: a confirming trace would contain an unknown/non-string `research.current`, eligible pack research, labs reporting missing packs, and then show this harness selecting a valid research and raising lab consumption/score. If all seeds always provide a named active research and lab starvation instead follows missing inputs, quota exhaustion, or power, the robustness change will have no effect and the hypothesis is refuted. The candidate should otherwise retain the leader’s training score because the usual string-valued path selects the same priorities.
