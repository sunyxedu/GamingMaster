Hypothesis: the leader’s nearby-placement recovery can repair a failed building operation, but the repair is only half applied. `local_repair` changes the queued task coordinate while `furnace_ores`, `chest_items`, and `green_roles` remain keyed by the old coordinate. On a seed where a tree, shoreline edge, or collision rejects one placement, the controller can therefore continue spending turns with a furnace that is never refilled, a coal chest that is never drained, or a logistics assembler that is treated as a red-science machine. This explains why the healthy training path is flat while the policy is vulnerable to unseen geography.

What changed vs. the parent: retain the parent’s deterministic build compiler, quota-aware transfers, research normalization, and bounded Astra repair. When a placement is shifted, reconcile every coordinate-indexed role map atomically with the task: furnace ore assignment, coal chest assignment, and green assembler recipe assignment move to the new half-tile location. The existing retry budget still prevents repeated illegal placements, and the repaired task cursor is rewound so earlier successful operations are not replayed blindly.

Evidence that would confirm or refute it: a confirming trace will show a rejected `place` followed by `local_repair`, then a shifted machine whose source/output transfers and recipe match its new coordinate and whose score resumes increasing. If no placement fails on held-out maps, or if failures are caused by power/fluid topology rather than coordinate-key drift, this change should preserve the parent’s score while leaving the hypothesis untested.
