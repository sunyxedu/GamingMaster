## Hypothesis

The leader's exact trade compiler has an incomplete action vocabulary: it trades only when a complete project is already fundable. On an economy with missing or weak resource production, surplus cards can therefore remain exposed to sevens even when a partial maritime exchange would store useful purchasing power. This proposal tests **inventory conversion before complete funding**, retaining the leader's opening, project values, tactical ledger and purchases. It does not forbid development cards or wait instead of a funded productive build.

Concrete evidence from `0009/trace_seed102.jsonl`: line 33 ends turn 22 holding `[0,1,4,0,2]`; line 35 ends turn 26 with `[0,2,4,0,4]`, in WOOD/BRICK/SHEEP/WHEAT/ORE order. Lines 36–40 then discard ORE, SHEEP, SHEEP, ORE, BRICK. Trading four sheep for wheat before passing would have reduced the exposed hand and retained a required city input, although whether that counterfactual wins is unknown. The episode loses at 6 true VP. In seed 103, line 82 ends turn 67 holding six wheat, two brick and no other resources; lines 83–86 discard four wheat. These are legal end-turn failures, not execution errors. Prior full-build forecast experiments 0011/0012 did not improve training win rate; this experiment changes which intermediate transactions the compiler can perform, rather than merely estimating the same complete purchases more accurately.

## Change

Retain 0009 as the parent. Add a bounded public-information inventory planner at otherwise idle normal-turn decisions. It enumerates single deficit-directed partial maritime exchanges and compares their future project completion against keeping the original hand, using 64 shared synthetic dice worlds over five personal rounds, whole maritime lots, number-correlated production and repeated seven-discards. It never consumes a required target input as trade surplus. Current actions must match the live legal menu; futures are estimates, not engine simulations or hidden-hand reconstructions. A meaningful improvement over both holding for that goal and the best alternative project is required. Complete funded projects, tactical cards and the parent's paid route steps retain priority. Forecasts do not compound production after the target purchase: this is a comparison of intermediate resource inventories, not another general investment scheduler.

Each accepted partial exchange records the intended project, imported resource, expected completion window and exact resource delta. On every subsequent observation the target is revalidated, progress is checked, and a missed conversion window or lost target is reported to Astra with the current evidence. Horizons with insufficient completion probability are explicitly censored. Additional imports or target changes cannot extend an existing deadline. While an inventory plan is active, repeated discards use its fixed resource recipe, matching the forecast; otherwise the parent's discard policy remains. Competing purchases are logged as reallocation, and imported cards are not permanently locked. An award's remaining route is refreshed after successful roads rather than misclassifying its changed path identifier as failure. Legal rejection, stale IDs, malformed model text and model timeouts retain local fallback. Inference is bounded above the archive's recent worst latency, with a 300-second backend timeout and a 210-second foreground wait. Model requests obsolete at the time of a new failure may be superseded once, without accumulating unbounded background work. The loop also reserves time before synchronous game advances and unwinds budget exceptions cleanly, following the contract repair in 0012.

## Confirmation / refutation

Support would be fewer avoidable large-hand passes and discards, partial imports converting into timely productive builds, and improved primary win rate with higher weak-episode true VP. Refute if early exchanges destroy useful flexibility, import resources that dice soon provide anyway, divert inputs from better projects, or miss their conversion windows without useful model recovery. Report win rate and mean terminal true VP separately. No training-seed conditions, hidden game state, engine imports, or held-out results are used.

## Validation

Final preflight passed: game_done, 108 controlled actions, no exception. No System-2 answer was returned in the dry run, so separate mocked checks cover arbitrary non-text/JSON/prose replies, TimeoutError and RuntimeError, a contradicted trade receipt, explicit conversion completion, a missed deadline with progress evidence, bounded stale-request replacement, and deadline admission. Three thousand randomized integer-funding checks agreed with the inherited exact maritime compiler. See `mechanics_checks.txt` and `preflight_result.json`.

Archived-state checks covered all 364 nonterminal decisions in the parent's four training traces. Every selected action was currently legal. The partial-trade planner activated in six states, including SHEEP→WHEAT in the seed-102 turn-22/26/34 inventories and WHEAT→WOOD at seed-103 turn 67. These are counterfactual choices, not replayed game outcomes. For example, the turn-26 city forecast's discounted completion utility increased from 0.546 to 0.620 and modeled discards fell from 6.42 to 3.69 cards; those are assumptions-dependent synthetic predictions, not observed gains. All other fresh-state action choices match the parent's local policy. See `archive_checks.json` and `isolation_checks.json`.

A separate 512-world synthetic sample confirmed a positive discounted-completion delta for all six selected exchanges (0.092–0.151), with 2.75–4.23 fewer modeled discarded cards. This reduces concern that those choices are artifacts of the planner's 64-world sample; it does not validate the shared assumptions about theft, robber release, future bank liquidity or competition. See `sampling_checks.json`.

Dev evaluation 1, seed 301: **win rate 1/1, terminal true VP 10**, 71 controlled actions, 53 completed normal turns, 427 seconds, two System-2 requests, one answered, no failed call or episode error. The second request was cancelled normally when the game ended. No partial inventory conversion was selected, so this verifies preservation of the inherited winning path rather than supporting the hypothesis. After loading this run, small verification refinements limited forecasts to one immediately executable exchange, labeled censored horizons, and made award-route monitoring semantic; no opening, purchasing or tactical-card values changed.

Dev evaluation 2, seed 302: **win rate 0/1, terminal true VP 9 / public VP 8**, 97 controlled actions, 111 completed normal turns, 924 seconds, seven System-2 requests, six answered, no failed call or episode error. At turn 38 the planner exchanged four WHEAT for one BRICK toward a two-road award project. Its completion probability was only 0.734 within five rounds, explicitly censored; the public road race subsequently required a longer route. The agent reallocated resources to development cards and missed the turn-58 window. At turn 62 the monitor logged the original forecast, current empty hand and `ctx.progress()` evidence, and a fresh Astra request included that failure. Astra replied: “Use Monopoly on sheep, trade surplus for brick, then settle wheat-rich vertex 2; abandon brick-starved road plan.” The harness played SHEEP Monopoly at turn 62, built settlement 2 at turn 74, and built cities at turns 86, 98 and 102. This verifies that failure evidence reaches a new model plan and execution resumes; it does not establish that the partial trade helped.

A second partial exchange, four ORE for SHEEP at turn 106, targeted settlement 32. The next turn's resources instead bought a development card worth one hidden VP, reaching 9 before ORANGE won. Neither original inventory target completed: the first failed its window and the second was censored by game end after reallocation. The development run therefore gives mixed evidence: the new actions are legal and recovery works, but it does not demonstrate improved conversion speed or win rate. An opponent-aware award horizon and explicit valuation of reallocation remain useful next hypotheses; no weights were retuned to this dev result.

Across the two permitted dev runs: **win rate 1/2; mean terminal true VP 9.5; mean composite score 0.595**, zero episode errors. The final harness was loaded for the second run and was not changed afterward. These are development results, not training leaderboard results. No full evaluation or held-out results were accessed.
