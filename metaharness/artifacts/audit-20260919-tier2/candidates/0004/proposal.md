# 0004 — Learn effects from transitions while Astra compiles

Performance parent: `0002-previous_astra_winner`. Implementation starting point: `0003`, with its failed effect-classification bridge replaced and its compiler interface repaired. This is one candidate, not an evaluated improvement.

## Hypothesis and archive evidence

The winner loses reward opportunities during cold compilation. Its worst episode, seed 0, survives all 120 ticks with zero stale ticks but scores only 7. In `../0002-previous_astra_winner/trace_seed0.jsonl:166`, `policy_installed` occurs at `"frame":54`, `"clock":27.00787091255188`. Rewards occur at ticks 3, 62, 78, 94, 104, 111 and 117. The second compilation arrives at line 367 with `"episode_active":false`; line 368 records `"search_frames":0`. The other two seeds also have no search frames. The manifest resets memory per episode, so this late work cannot benefit the next seed.

Jev can identify meanings more reliably than it can reason about action effects. The winner's seed 0 line 6 chooses the default action with confidence 0.39, while classifying the safety map with confidence 0.99. Candidate 0003 confirms the semantic part: its worst episode, seed 2, binds the safety map and objective residual at frame 2 (lines 8–9), with confidence 1.0 and 0.99 respectively. But line 14's effect answers have probabilities 0.49, 0.71, 0.57 and 0.61 for the four movement actions. None passes the existing 0.80 probability / 0.70 confidence gates. Line 16 consequently logs `"effects":{}`. This happens in every seed; lowering the gates would turn uncertainty into control without additional evidence.

0003 also has a concrete compiler contract defect. Seed 2 line 243 returns `a==f[1]`, `o['danger'][x[0]]` and `f[0][x[0]][0]`. The runtime stores features only by name. Line 247 rejects the result with `"error":"KeyError: 1"` at frame 80. The repair returns only after termination; line 369 reports `"fallback":120,"binding":0,"compiled":0` and score 0. Seeds 0 and 1 suffer the same named-versus-positional mismatch, installing repairs only at frames 105 and 94.

**Testable hypothesis:** after Jev binds safety and an observable objective error, code can identify useful action effects from repeated public transitions before Astra finishes. This should produce earlier reward without requiring Jev to calculate coordinate effects or weakening its confidence gates. Tolerating both feature-reference conventions should separately eliminate the demonstrated repair delay.

## Procedure

1. Immediately post the action-spec default. Start one Astra compilation and one asynchronous Jev discovery batch. Discover candidate fields by shape only: action-indexed Boolean dictionaries and short numeric vectors. Jev classifies mask polarity and identifies an existing signed objective error whose zero represents the objective. Absolute positions and other quantities do not qualify. The question requires comparable units and an objective stationary between reward events, and includes `none`. Both confidence and probability gates remain unchanged.
2. During cold start, sample actions certified by a learned mask, preferring those with fewer observations. Record residual differences only for adjacent control intervals within 25% of one tick. Each action gets at most four identification samples; the overall identification sample budget is four times the action count. There is no action-name interpretation and no assumed unit movement or dimension count beyond the discovered vector shape.
3. Promote a nonzero additive effect after at least two identical differences constituting at least two-thirds of its samples. Recompute the predicted residual and its sum of absolute components on every live observation, choosing the lowest cost among currently certified actions with learned effects. Unobserved actions have no invented effect. The identification phase ends when available actions have sufficient evidence or the sampling budget is exhausted.
4. Audit learned effects on subsequent unambiguous transitions, including after compilation. An unexplained mismatch immediately revokes and disables that action's empirical effect for the episode. If the prediction reaches zero, skip that audit because the objective may have changed. Ambiguous timing produces no training example. A missing residual disables this bridge. These are empirical consistency checks, not a proof of globally constant dynamics.
5. Astra compiles a small expression policy from the actual public rules and observation. Features support both named reads (`f['name']`, `x['name']`) and positional reads (`f[0]`, `x[0]`); serialized feature facts remain named. Validate against initial and recent observations before installation. Restore the winner's allowance for a cheap continuation-quality cue, without requiring a transition simulator or search.
6. The compiled policy supplies safety, utilities, a summary, and a small regime context. With two modes, Jev receives that context and concrete mode criteria in one typed question with a no-match option. A selected mode needs probability >=0.70, confidence >=0.60, the same revision/context/allowed actions, and age <=4 ticks. Code recomputes utilities on current observations. A one-mode policy needs no repeated classification calls. Jev's discovery remains a substantive control input during cold start.
7. Without an applicable empirical bridge, retain shielded fallback and confidence-gated direct decisions. Direct decisions require exact observation equality and age <=2 ticks. Return to Astra only after policy validation/runtime failure, with the offending output and error, within the four-call budget. Remove the winner's unconditional second world-model pass.

The control loop never waits for a model before posting the current action. It uses `ctx.wait_for_tick()` between iterations. Every `ctx.spawn` future is joined on normal or exceptional exit; inference exceptions, including `ModelUnavailable`, propagate. Results received during final draining are not installed. There is no cross-episode policy or effect cache.

## Measurement and falsification

Primary comparison remains mean score, then stale ticks, then S2 calls. Check the mechanism separately:

- `semantic_binding` should precede `dynamics_audit` events with `outcome="learned"`, followed by `control` events with source `binding` well before `policy_installed`. `jev_binding` marks action changes relative to the shielded fallback; `jev_exploration` marks identification interventions separately. Discovery without interventions does not confirm the hypothesis.
- Compare reward earned before first policy installation and time to first reward. More reward only after installation is evidence for compiler repair or policy differences, not empirical warm-up.
- Compare first-response acceptance, compiler rejections and installation time against 0003. Against 0002, the compatibility fix alone should have little effect: its archived first policies already used named references.
- Refute the dynamics hypothesis if discovery abstains, effect learning is late, audits revoke frequently, or sampled actions cost more reward than subsequent empirical control recovers. Earlier deaths would be a serious regression because the winner survived every training episode.
- `control` logs shield/direct/empirical/compiled alternatives and actual intervention labels; `coverage` aggregates source frames and control changes. Lower S1/S2 counts by themselves are not evidence of improved reward control.

These changes are not perfectly isolated: the interface repair, removal of unused world compilation, and cheap continuation cue can independently affect results. The timestamps and source/intervention logs are necessary to distinguish them.

## Limits and local validation

This optional bridge requires observable, sufficiently stable objective error and a meaningful safety map. Games without them use the generic Astra compiler. Repeated samples can still overgeneralize a context-dependent effect; immediate legality does not guarantee future survival. Greedy residual reduction can enter cycles or traps, and active identification can sacrifice early rewards. Timing checks may discard useful evidence under scheduling jitter. Before the first safety interpretation arrives, even the action-spec default may be unsafe.

Local validation used only this archive and mocked interfaces, with no backend calls or performance experiments. All 399 archived reactive-policy/observation combinations from 0002 and 0003 resolve successfully, including 0003's three rejected first policies. Checks cover named/positional feature serialization, renamed-field shape discovery, both confidence gates, repeated-effect promotion, objective-boundary handling and revocation. Interface stubs exercise empirical/Astra/mode interventions and verify joining every future exactly once on normal termination, early termination, S1 unavailability, S2 unavailability and simultaneous unavailability. No game implementation or held-out game was inspected or tested.
