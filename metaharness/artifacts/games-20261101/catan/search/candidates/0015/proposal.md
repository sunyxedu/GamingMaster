## Hypothesis

The inherited project values reward a site's weighted pips without pricing the production bottleneck of the whole economy. More accurate funding forecasts cannot repair a bad investment objective. This experiment replaces local production rewards with the marginal reduction in a public-information, complete-to-ten resource budget. It keeps parent 0002's opening and exact execution compiler, rather than refining leader 0009 or adding the parallel proposals' partial-trade/staged-road actions.

Concrete evidence: leader 0009 seed 102, trace line 19 (controlled step 14, turn 10), upgrades vertex 10. The pre-action production per personal round in WOOD/BRICK/SHEEP/WHEAT/ORE order is [0, .444, .556, .222, .889]. Vertex 10 adds [0,0,5,0,5] pips; vertex 5 adds [0,4,0,2,3]. Both cities are immediately legal and cost the same, but the parent values city 10 at 13.35 and city 5 at 12.91. It doubles sheep/ore while leaving wheat at two pips. At line 35 it passes with [0,2,4,0,4], then discards on the next seven; it eventually loses at 6 true VP. This does not prove city 5 wins, but it gives an explicit ranking prediction. The other leader loss reaches only 7 VP after upgrading both original sites and no ordinary settlement. Earlier timing experiments 0011/0012 retained the same local project values and did not improve training win rate.

## Change

Replace the midgame project objective with an economy-wide remaining-victory calculator. Enumerate feasible mixtures of future settlements, cities, Army, a currently reachable Road award, and expected development VP. Price the aggregate resource demand through the observed production vector and maritime rates. Compare each proposed investment by how much its actual production/port/point changes reduce that remaining budget's funding time. For immediately funded development purchases, compare the expected remaining budget after paying exact trade/build costs with the budget when keeping the current hand; purchases that destroy estimated value may be declined. This is a long-run fluid economic valuation, explicitly not an integer-card readiness forecast; current affordability still uses the exact inherited compiler and current legal IDs. No hidden hands, deck order, engine code, seed-conditioned choices, or game-source imports are used.

Astra sees the best remaining-victory recipes, marginal investment effects, and uncertainty assumptions. The executor verifies every action and reads ctx.progress(). A production investment that fails to produce subsequent score growth within its recorded window, an invalidated route, contradictory award prediction, or rejected action supplies concrete evidence for Astra to revise the plan. Current menu IDs remain authoritative, and malformed/late model replies and timeouts retain a local fallback. Model timeout is 300 seconds, with a bounded 210-second foreground wait, both above the recent 134-second maximum.

## Confirmation and refutation

First check the specific economic ranking: prefer city 5 to city 10 in the archived seed-102 turn-10 state. Support then requires more balanced conversion capacity, productive ordinary expansion or cities, and improved win rate and weak-seed terminal true VP. Refute if the long-run budgets overvalue slow infrastructure, expected development outcomes misprice Army/card routes, or better-looking throughput fails to become actual points before opponents finish. Report win rate separately from mean terminal true VP. Exact execution success alone does not validate the strategic hypothesis.

## Model assumptions and execution details

The budget enumerates up to four mutually nonadjacent currently reachable expansion sites and the remaining city pieces. Future cities may free settlement pieces. Costs include access roads, observed port rates, and enough expected cards to supply both missing hidden VP and needed Knights. Army ties retain the incumbent and needed Knights require separate turns to play. Expected card outcomes use the published 20% VP / 56% Knight / 24% utility prior; the utility branch receives one round-equivalent of economic credit. Those are estimates, not observations of the remaining deck. Future compounding is excluded from the budget, while the candidate investment's own production is added exactly by observed pips. Future rival expansion, award defense, bank shortages, robber persistence, and card variance are not simulated. This makes the economic clock a marginal valuation instrument rather than a finish-time forecast.

The exact inherited compiler still handles funding, actions, and whole-project transactions. A hand-aware budget includes both missing resources and any surplus hand cards at their observed maritime rates. Development is the only uncertain purchase subject to the net-cost savings check; guaranteed building/award points retain their execution priority. A completed purchase ends its transaction so subsequent purchases compete afresh. Bounded model inference permits one obsolete pending request to be superseded by new failure evidence, never unbounded background work. The loop reserves at least 45 seconds (adapted to observed step duration) before synchronous opponent advancement. A six-own-round conversion review is a failure signal, not a claim that the budget predicts that deadline.
