### General lessons

- Frame-rate ownership was confirmed: synchronous 0000/0001/0002 had stale fractions **0.8896/0.999673/1.0**, while asynchronous 0003/0004/0005 had **0.000119/0.0000988/0.000122**; low staleness alone did not ensure a win.

- Optional model mechanisms were not tested: 0003 logged `NO_FEATURE` on **2,?** calls, 0005 used `use_feature=false` in **3** Astra plans, and 0004's observed style was predominantly `CENTER`; no accepted FIRE pulse appears in the winning traces.

- The point traces repeatedly show `phase=serve_delay`, `serve_countdown_frames=63`, `ball_x=156.5`, and null velocity after a miss; stale point sprites must be ignored, and automatic serves require no FIRE. Direction mapping also matters: `RIGHT` is screen-y up and `LEFT` is screen-y down.

### General lessons

### General lessons

- The local frame-rate-owned reflected-intercept loop remains the confirmed base: 0009 and 0010 preserved **20,20,21** with stale fractions below **0.00016**; the score gains did not require model answers on the action path.
- The feature mechanism remains untested causally: Generation 3 produced **2** trial-arm events, **0** FIRE actions, and no accepted `USE` vote; all three candidates kept `use_feature=false` in the effective Astra plans.
- The parser repair was confirmed as plumbing, not as a safe policy improvement: 0011 made `EDGE` replans appear in traces and changed seed 1 from the parent's **20:0 deadline** pattern to **21:0**, but seed 0 changed from **20:0** to **17** with **4** opponent points.
- Asynchronous calls protected frame ownership: S1→act stayed about **2.60–2.63%**, S2→act about **3.69–4.03e-5**, and every candidate had no evaluation error; the remaining costs were **1**, **2**, and **0** deadline episodes for 0009/0010/0011 plus failed Astra replans, not stale ticks.
- Still untested are a legal, accepted feature pulse on Robot Pong 1 and a measured contact-offset/edge-sign experiment; traces currently show policy labels and outcomes, but not a causal return-angle measurement.

- **Remaining headroom:** The best candidate `0006` has mean components **own_points 20.67**, **opponent_points 0.33**, **return 20.33**, **win 0.67**, from returns **20,20,21**; the game description makes **21:0**, return **21**, win **1**, and `game_done` achievable in every episode. The largest score-component gap is return (**0.67**), caused by the **20:0** rally that reached the **53,999-tick** deadline without point 21; opponent scoring (**0.33**) is the smaller gap.
