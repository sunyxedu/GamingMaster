Hypothesis: the autonomous explorer's limiting factor is a missing distinction between safe progress, transient obstruction, and irreversible combat risk. More throughput alone can erase the entire measured return. A controller that keeps terrain separate from occupants, checks every action against current danger, and ends an unsafe run before lethal continuation should improve the weak seeds while still exploiting deterministic descent.

Evidence: in candidate 0002 seed 1106, the final tty says "125 ... died ... level 3. Killed by an acid blob", but episode_end reports score 0. Candidate 0003 seed 1107 similarly reports score 0 after a displayed score of 10; its step-550 checkpoint has HP 5/10 at game turn 308 and action move ne, followed by death to a goblin. These are observations of this interface, not an assumption that ordinary NetHack death always destroys score. Candidate 0002 seed 1108 ends after 1,069 commands at time 336 with "It's a wall." The leader's seed-1107 request at clock 735.60 contains "As you kick the door, it crashes open!", then another kick and "Really attack the little dog?" twice; the pet at (70,12) obscures a remembered closed door. Commands, traversed geometry, occupants, and game-time progress require separate evidence.

Changes versus parent: replace free-form action queues with one-step risk checks, repair geometric beliefs from action outcomes even when a creature obscures the square, make goals persistent but bounded, and use danger-aware routing. Passive/unknown creatures are obstacles rather than mandatory fights. Combat uses a health reserve and recent damage evidence; safe retreat and regeneration take priority. If the restricted controls provide no credible safe continuation at critical HP, return normally rather than execute an inevitably dangerous fallback. Astra gives bounded tactical classifications and strategic corrections at unresolved decisions; routine exploration and recovery remain deterministic. Its reply never bypasses local movement and HP constraints. Every model failure is caught.

Confirm if: early deaths and repeated no-progress actions fall, low-scoring seeds retain positive scores, and more reachable stairs are collected before serious injury. Logs must distinguish preserved-score stops, safe rests, combat decisions, blocked edges, door outcomes, and advice calls. Refute if: the health reserve terminates recoverable runs too early, avoiding creatures prevents reachable stairs, or efficient exploration still produces low scores despite surviving. Compare score and depth as well as death counts; an early safe return alone is not evidence of stronger play.
