Hypothesis: the remaining low-score failures are caused by continuing a healthy first-floor walk after progress has stopped, which eventually exposes the player to an unseen lethal encounter. Candidate 0012 and its descendants already have movement, prompt, pet, cycle, and HP gates, but they do not treat a score plateau as a survival signal. The strongest concrete example is the 0015 trace for seed 1104: score reaches 8 at step 59, remains 8 through step 314 with full HP (16/16 in the harness checkpoint), and `move sw` at step 315 ends the game and resets terminal score to 0. Seed 1108 similarly remains at score 4 through 403 actions and returns while still on depth 1. The useful intervention is to preserve a small score once exploration is no longer paying off, rather than spend more turns on a low-value route.

What changed vs. the parent: I retained the parent’s deterministic map bookkeeping, transient-pet and cycle repair, guarded movement, bounded unknown-melee probe, food/prompt state machine, and cold Astra escape hatch. I added a score-only plateau counter keyed by dungeon level. At depth 1, when score is at most 10, the score has not increased for 180 observations, HP is above the computed reserve, and no monster is visible, the harness returns with a logged `low-score first-floor plateau safety stop`. The gate is intentionally narrow: it does not fire after a score increase, on deeper levels, near a visible threat, or while injured, and it leaves higher-scoring long plateaus (for example seed 1102’s 62→152 gap) unchanged.

Evidence that would confirm the hypothesis: on seeds like 1104, the run should end before the lethal late move and retain score 8; on 1108 it should stop earlier at the same score rather than spend hundreds of redundant turns. The intervention is refuted if it stops before a first-floor descent or score increase on healthy low-score runs, or if terminal deaths remain at the same plateau despite the gate. The trace should show the new stop reason and no action posted after the qualifying observation.
