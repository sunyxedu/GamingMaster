### General lessons for next proposers

- **Confirmed: fresh deterministic motor plumbing matters.** 0008 and 0011 achieved 0.589% and 0.345% stale ticks respectively. 0011's improvement demonstrates that changing local heading selection can outperform adding a more elaborate global frontier structure. Preserve the committed motor and its asynchronous execution rather than reverting to long model-controlled actions.
- **Refuted as a sufficient improvement: frontier bookkeeping alone.** 0009 scored 6.680 and 0010 scored 5.837, versus 0008's 9.382. More explicit branch memory and keyed exploration did not outperform the simpler sweep motor. The 0010 prediction about key milestones and USE-at-barrier opportunities remains unconfirmed.
- **Confirmed: local geometric revisitation is actionable.** 0011 improved mean score by 16.8% over its parent while reducing stale ticks by 0.244 percentage points. Heading visits are useful when they influence actual motor decisions, not merely question ordering.
- **Untested: reliable exit discovery.** All three candidates recorded zero demonstrated exit success in the leaderboard composition. The absence of visible exit labels makes interaction and sector-transition hypotheses difficult to validate. A high exploration score is not proof that the exit was found.
- **Plumbing remains costly.** 0009 had 6 failed S1 calls out of 2,275; 0010 had 5 out of 3,524; 0011 had 6 failed S1 and 3 failed S2 calls out of 3,059 and 17 respectively. S2 latency is approximately 65 seconds plus 4 ms per output token, so bounded outputs and explicit timeout budgets remain important. Low stale-tick percentages do not eliminate model-call failures.

- **Remaining headroom:** 0011's mean score composition is armor 0.64, health 0.81, item progress 0.36, kill progress 0.37, normalized progress 7.85, remaining ammo 0.76, secret progress 0.03, time tiebreak 0.90, and exit success 0.00. Its total is 10.96, with normalized progress providing most of the score. The game objective of reaching the exit remains unachieved: even the best episode, seed 1008 at 18.43, ended at the deadline, while the worst ended game_done at 3.21. The largest remaining objective gap is **exit success (0.00)**, followed by secret progress (0.03). The next generation should prioritize converting exploration into actual level completion rather than merely increasing explored territory, kills or collected items.
