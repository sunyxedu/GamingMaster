Hypothesis: the remaining losses are often payload and action-capacity failures inside an otherwise legal engine. The public race lock in 0006 reacts to Province ownership, but it can still choose a long terminal/draw shell whose money density cannot reliably reach $8. In the -20 `chapel_engine` / SM-1 trace of candidate 0008, the agent repeatedly bought Laboratory/Market while it had no Gold and only a thin Silver payload; by turn 14 it had spent $11 on more terminals, then converted the large action chain into only one Province. The trace also shows repeated terminal plays after action support had been consumed. This suggests a structural bottleneck before race timing: action capacity and payload must be jointly sufficient.

What changed versus parent: candidate 0011 derives from 0006's public-belief rollout and race-aware executor, but adds two local invariants shared by real choices and rollouts. First, when no Gold exists and a $6 purchase is affordable, the buy policy strongly reserves that purchase for Gold until an estimated payload anchor (Treasures plus documented action money) is adequate; extra Laboratory, Market, Village, Festival and Smithy values are reduced while Silver density is still low. Province remains higher priority when actually affordable. Second, action selection detects a hand with more terminal Actions than remaining Actions and no draw/action-support card, and declines the terminal chain instead of spending the last Action on collision. All prompt IDs, nested choices, scoring baskets and model-error recovery remain owned by code.

Astra still receives the parent's optional bounded strategic-policy request, but advice cannot bypass the payload/action gates. The forward model applies the same `buy_values` and `action_value` helpers, so sampled comparisons test the new invariants rather than an execution-only patch. No seed, hidden order, kingdom identifier or simulator implementation is used.

Evidence that would confirm the hypothesis: fewer purchases of engine terminals before the first Gold, fewer terminal-collision action sequences, earlier reliable $8 turns, and improved margins on Chapel/SM boards without increasing illegal actions or truncation. Refutation would be unchanged payload composition despite the gate, or lower margins because Gold displaces a genuinely winning draw engine; traces would show the controller reaching $8 already and still losing on Province timing. In that case the payload diagnosis is wrong and the public race intervention should be revisited. The change is intentionally conservative: it does not force Gold when Province is affordable and does not alter Gardens-specific behavior.
