Hypothesis: the remaining low episodes are caused by starvation converting a recoverable expedition into a death spiral. Candidate 0006 already added a short hostile retreat, but its worst trace still shows food falling from 4 at step 180 to 0 by step 277; it then spends roughly 100 ticks moving around the lower-left boundary while health declines from 6 to 0. The common failure is that normal progression continues after the reserve is too small for a safe return.

What changed versus 0006: I added a code-only food reserve mode. At food <=3 (or <=4 with health <=5), the controller suspends crafting, mining, and Astra objectives for 90 ticks. It tries a visible/remembered ripe plant or visible cow first, and otherwise explores the known walkable frontier to reveal food. The lock is released only after food reaches 7, so one successful eat cannot immediately send the player back into a long expedition. Existing hostile panic, path search, recipe verification, and asynchronous Astra consultation remain unchanged.

The hypothesis is supported if the training traces show fewer food=0 transitions, fewer health-zero endings after a long expedition, and improved worst-seed score without reducing early dependency achievements. It is refuted if food-lock exploration strands the player in resource-poor regions, consumes the episode before iron/diamond work, or leaves deaths unchanged when no food is visible. A confirming trace should show a low-food trigger followed by food collection and release at a reserve of at least 7; a refuting trace will show repeated food-search actions with no newly observed cow/plant and no score benefit.
