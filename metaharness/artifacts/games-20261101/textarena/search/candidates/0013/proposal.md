## Hypothesis

The leader's low outcomes are residual decision uncertainty, not execution errors. In `0006/trace_seed13.jsonl`, `[5 3 2 4]` receives `0 black, 3 white`, then `[5 2 3 6]` receives `1 black, 2 white`. The 18-code final posterior chooses `[4 2 3 5]`, logs expected score 0.5416667 and solve probability zero, and realizes 0.5. Every verification reports `issue: null`. Seed 11 similarly realizes 0.625 against a logged final expectation of 0.725. Two other Mastermind episodes solve, producing the leader's 0.640625 suite mean. These four observations do not establish an expectation above the public-rule Bayes optimum.

Hypothesis: the parent's one randomly relabeled lexical policy unnecessarily restricts the support of optimal decisions. Independently mixing all exactly tied actions at each observed posterior can spread residual risk across the full optimal-action set, rather than correlate every decision through one digit permutation. It preserves optimal mean and solve probability under the uniform distinct-code model. Whether it improves the payoff distribution or conditional worst-secret expectation is an empirical question, not a claimed consequence of randomization. In particular, an independent mixture cannot reduce variance merely by averaging actions: the game still receives one sampled action.

## Change

Retain the leader's exhaustive two-stage numeric-reward search, public belief model, TicTacToe negamax, one-step execution and Astra recovery. Replace the sampled digit permutation plus lexical tie order with a behavioral strategy:

- Draw the opening uniformly from the 360 legal guesses.
- Enumerate every legal second probe and all of its feedback partitions. Retain every maximizer of exact expected reward, then solve probability; uniformly sample a probe from this set.
- Store each selected probe's contingent branches as a belief, the complete set of reward-and-solve-optimal final actions, and an integer payoff certificate. At the actual final posterior, recompute the certificate and uniformly sample one of its tied final actions.

The sampling is independent private entropy, never episode or environment seeds. Nothing from archived secrets enters the runtime. All actions remain feedback dependent. The new policy is equivariant in distribution under digit relabeling because its objective and uniform tie distributions are invariant; root digit-map sampling is no longer needed to obtain equal ex-ante expectations across fixed secrets. The expected uniform-code mean should remain 1915/2880 = 0.6649305556 for Mastermind, or 0.5824652778 for the balanced suite. This is not a claim to exceed that optimum or reproduce the leader's favorable training realization.

Effect checks use `ctx.progress()`, legal-action consumption, native move count, feedback persistence, posterior consistency, horizon and terminal numeric reward. A missing contingent branch or altered optimal-action certificate invalidates compiled control. Astra receives the public observation, rule description, legal actions and failure evidence and returns one legal move; its effects are checked again. Timeout, backend errors and arbitrary replies use a legal fallback. No normal-path model call is added.

## Confirmation and refutation

Enumerate the canonical opening's complete stochastic tree using exact rational weights for every tied probe and final action, without looking at any seed or hidden environment state. Compare the exact score histogram, mean, solve mass and weak-outcome probability against the parent's public all-code result. Measure whether mixing spreads conditional payoffs for each final posterior. Refute the mean-preservation claim on any deviation from the known reward optimum; refute the risk-improvement claim if weak-outcome mass or variance rises. A native dev score is only an integration result, not evidence to select a favorable random policy. Required preflight and injected recovery stand-ins check the contract independently of scores.

## Validation

No full training, validation or held-out evaluation was run during development. Completed checks:

- Required preflight passed: TicTacToe draw, four actions, zero model calls, no exception. Normal preflight cannot exercise recovery, so that path has separate fault injection.
- One native dev evaluation, `dev/20261031-132415`, returned 0.5 on each of 210 and 211, with zero calls, errors or rejected actions. All seven `verified_step` records have `issue: null`. Mastermind took about 1.73 seconds; all 359 probes were evaluated. The final 20-code posterior had 12 tied optimal actions and expected reward 0.575; the sampled final action realized 0.5. The digest and both traces were inspected. No second dev evaluation was used to seek a favorable random realization.
- `check_policy.py` independently verifies all 129,600 public feedback/reward entries, then integrates the complete stochastic tree for all 360 abstract codes with rational probabilities. It independently checks 2,928 terminal optimal-action-set certificates against direct payoff summation. The exact mean remains **383/576 = 0.6649305556** and solve probability remains **53/360**. Fractional expected counts at rewards `(0.25, 0.375, 0.5, 0.625, 0.75, 1)` are `(10/3, 191/6, 238/3, 167/2, 109, 53)` out of 360. They are probability masses, not integer outcomes from one sampled tree.
- Below-0.5 probability falls from the parent's 36/360 = 10% to **211/2160 = 9.76852%**. Variance falls from **0.034299648** to **0.034111569** (about 0.55%). The realized floor remains 0.25. These are modest improvements; they do not dominate candidate 0010's explicit threshold insurance and do not imply a higher training mean.
- `conditional_comparison.json` compares the parent's two equally likely residual digit maps after the concrete opening `[1 2 3 4]` with the new full mixture. Conditional on opening feedback `(0,3)`, the minimum expected continuation over each consistent fixed secret increases from **0.375 to 0.5859375**, while the conditional average stays **0.61079545**. For `(0,2)`, the same minimum rises from **0.3125 to 0.59375**. All ten non-solving first-feedback branches improve this conditional expected floor. This is the meaningful structural effect: the exposed opening does not pin later decisions to a tiny correlated family. It is neither a per-play reward floor nor additional information about the hidden code.
- `check_symmetry.py` compares all optimal probe supports and their final-action sets under a digit swap and six-cycle (generators of the full label group). All **5,856** branch comparisons preserve beliefs, action supports and payoff certificates. Because sampling is uniform on each support, this verifies distributional equivariance for those transformed trees and supports the symmetry argument for equal ex-ante expectations across fixed secrets.
- `check_recovery.py` verifies all **787** TicTacToe terminal paths against arbitrary opponent moves with no losses. Seven injected faults (rejection, no observation change, action exception, changed feedback format, model timeout, backend error, garbage response) invoke the budgeted model interface and finish in at most four actions.
- `check_certificates.py` tests five corruptions (belief, action set, reward, solve count, missing branch) crossed with legal replies, garbage and timeouts. All **15** cases detect the discrepancy, hand evidence to Astra, finish in three actions and incur no invalid moves. The model cannot bypass the final legal-action gate.

The mean-preservation and conditional-support hypotheses are supported by public-rule enumeration. Only a small distributional improvement is established, and this single native dev run shows no score improvement. The policy remains stochastic; independent future training samples may rank it above or below its parent. No runtime decision uses archived scores, evaluator seeds, hidden-state access, or source outside the allowed archive.
