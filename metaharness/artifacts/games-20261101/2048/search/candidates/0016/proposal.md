Hypothesis: the leader's resource beam preserves mass and empty cells but its leaf geometry is orientation agnostic. The cached `shape()` scores smoothness and pair potential over fixed up/left lines, so a complete path can repeatedly score well while moving the largest tile away from a stable corner. This is consistent with the plateaued 3056 episodes: seed 23 ends with 256 on the second row, seed 37 with 256 in the left interior, and seed 59 with a strong top row, yet all have mass 470–478 and max tile 256. These are verified trajectories with no rule or replay errors, so the suspected failure is strategic geometry rather than liveness.

Change versus 0013: retain its exact slide/score/spawn simulator, verified public RNG stream, occupancy and cursor diversity, complete incumbent suffix, bounded search, asynchronous Astra supervision, progress based replanning, and all-spawn fallback. Add a persistent corner anchor selected from the observed opening board. The anchor has hysteresis: it remains fixed while its corner holds the maximum or a substantial fraction of it, and changes only after visible damage. Beam and greedy completion leaves now add an anchor-relative snake score: the anchor tile is rewarded, reversals away from the corner are penalized, and adjacent equal ranks remain useful for merges. Astra receives the current anchor and can diagnose whether the board has damaged it; model replies remain optional guidance and cannot replace verified actions. The anchor term is only a search guide; final acceptance remains exact merge score plus max tile.

Evidence that would confirm or refute it: higher terminal merge score or a 512 tile on seeds where 0013 leaves a 256 off-corner, with all 200 actions and replay checks intact, supports the corner hypothesis. If the anchor reduces mass acquisition, fills the board, or lowers score while max tiles remain 256, the geometry penalty is too strong or the resource ceiling dominates. If the observed maximum repeatedly leaves the anchor, the hysteresis/recovery events should make that visible rather than silently continuing a stale plan. No seed-specific paths or hidden state are used.
