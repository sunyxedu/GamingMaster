Change vs. parent: retain exact local slide accounting, 2/4 chance nodes, bounded search, and per-step observation checks. Commit to one inferred corner during search; penalize immediate loss of a 64+ anchor and let Astra revise the anchor, mode, or depth on review. The controller examines `ctx.progress()` for score stagnation, unchanged observations, and rejected actions, and checks every resulting board for exactly one legal spawn and the expected score/move change. All replies are optional and parsed defensively. The search is bounded to about one CPU second per action; model calls have a 150-second timeout, above the archive's 112-second worst.

Confirmation: the seed-independent controller finishes all 200 moves without errors and improves the minimum and mean scores over 0002, particularly on seeds whose largest tile otherwise drifts into the interior. Refutation: the fixed corner limits merge access, recovery reviews arrive too late to save damaged layouts, or scores remain flat despite fewer off-corner high tiles. Traces should distinguish a transition mismatch from legal but strategically poor moves.
