Hypothesis: the shallow corner expectimax in candidate 0001 leaves value on the table because it commits after effectively one future ply, while a fixed corner can become a liability after unlucky spawns. A bounded multi-ply controller that evaluates all legal actions under spawn uncertainty, and lets Astra switch between protecting space and harvesting merges, should improve both survival and score without depending on the public seed implementation.

What changed vs. the parent: the new harness uses a depth 2–3 expectimax with stratified spawn-position chance nodes, an orientation-independent snake score, smoothness, empty-cell count, and merge-pair potential. A one-second per-decision CPU guard prevents a model-selected depth from consuming the episode deadline. It starts with a balanced regime and asks Astra only for a mode/depth policy; it asks again when `ctx.progress()` reports stagnation, rejected actions, or verification disagreement. Every move is checked against the exact local slide and score/move deltas, and malformed or timed-out model replies leave safe defaults in place. The controller can therefore execute the whole 200-move episode while consuming only a handful of slow calls.
