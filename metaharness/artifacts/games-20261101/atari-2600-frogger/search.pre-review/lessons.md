- **0000, Jev-only baseline (no parent):** One System-1 `Choice` over the raw observation established a mean **6.00** with **2,194 S1 calls**, **89.6% stale ticks**, 4 deaths and 0 homes in every episode. The worst trace, seed 103, applied `UP` on 2,757/3,026 ticks (a maximum run of 1,239), reaching score 5 before deaths at ticks 1,262, 2,428, 2,628 and 2,840; the best, seed 113, applied `UP` on 3,395/3,593 ticks (maximum run 1,453) and still ended score 7 with no home.
- **0001, Astra-only baseline (no parent):** One slow System-2 call per decision confirmed the latency hypothesis: mean **1.00**, **19 S2 calls**, **99.98% stale**, 1 failed S2, and 0 homes. In worst seed 109, the first call returned empty, no action was ever posted, all 10,230 ticks were `NOOP`, and the episode ended score 0/no_frogs; best seed 107 posted only four actions, held its first `UP` for 3,693 ticks, reached score 2, then lost all four frogs.
- **0002, idle reference (no parent):** No model calls and no actions gave a clean plumbing reference but confirmed that idling earns nothing: mean **0.00**, **100% stale**, 0 homes and 4 deaths in every trace. Seed 101 is representative: 10,148 `NOOP` ticks, score 0, then `no_frogs`; safe syntax is not game progress.
- **0003, Jev-only refine of 0000:** Releasing a directional answer after one applied tick and inserting `NOOP` was confirmed as the right bridge mechanism: mean **12.00** versus 6.00, with **86.9% stale** and 1,592 S1 calls. Every trace shows isolated one-tick `UP` pulses (seed 109: 85 pulses, maximum directional run 1) instead of held directions; scores were 10–15. The hypothesis was only partially confirmed: all six still ended `no_frogs` with 4 deaths and 0 homes, and no lateral action appeared.
- **0004, rewrite of 0002 with an Astra planner card and pipelined typed pulse controller:** Freshly posting an action every tick drove stale ticks to **0%**, but the agreement/risk gates accepted too little Jev input (**8.3% Jev→act**); mean fell to **5.17** with **3,706 S1**, 12 S2, and 4 timed-out S2 calls. Best seed 109 had only 29 one-tick `UP` pulses amid 3,720 `NOOP`s and still had 4 deaths; worst seed 107 had 2 `UP` pulses amid 9,494 `NOOP`s and score 1. The planner/pulse hypothesis did not beat the released-hop controller and produced no home.
- **0005, crossover of 0000 and 0002:** Released-edge Jev control plus an asynchronous advisory supervisor achieved **99.3% Jev→act** but mostly acted on `NOOP`; mean was only **2.00**, with **90.2% stale**, 4 deaths and 0 homes in all episodes. Best seed 101 posted just 3 `UP` pulses and then ended score 3; worst seed 109 posted 4 pulses followed by an 8,153-tick `NOOP` run and ended score 1. The high `wait_is_safe`/`START` answers and negligible Astra influence refuted the claimed supervisor improvement.

### General lessons
- One-frame release is the only mechanism clearly confirmed: it changed directional runs from 1,239–1,453 frames to 1 frame and raised mean score by 6, but it did not solve hazard selection or home alignment.
- Freshness alone is not useful throughput: 0004 had 0 stale ticks but only 2–29 forward pulses per trace; 0005 had 99.3% Jev→act while nearly every accepted action was `NOOP`.
- System-2 cannot be the 60-Hz driver: the measured fit is **49.7 s + 11.2 ms/output token**; 0001 was 99.98% stale, 0004 timed out 4/12 calls, and 0004/0005 Astra actions were effectively zero.
- The remaining untested mechanism is verified Frogger perception and route choice: 0003–0005 produced only `UP`/`NOOP` actions, never reached a home, and relied on row/x fields explicitly marked as hypotheses rather than hazard/platform identities.
- Contract details cost episodes: without an explicit release, a 0.15–0.20-s Jev answer held `UP` for 1,000+ frames; with conservative gates, fallback `NOOP` let the timer consume all four frogs. Literal legal actions and clean failure handling prevented harness errors, but did not prevent game deaths.
- **Remaining headroom:** best 0003 reports mean `ale_reward=score=12`, `frogs_home=0`, `deaths=4`, `reserve_frogs=0`, `level=1`, `score_delta=0` (time bonus 357.67). Its six episodes all end `no_frogs`; against the stated 5 points per home and 100-point five-home bonus, home completion/level progression—not forward-hop credit—is the furthest-behind component.
